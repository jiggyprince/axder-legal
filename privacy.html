1. Who We Are

AXDER ONE GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED (RC 9698586) is a company registered in Nigeria. We build and operate Axder, an AI-powered business management application. Our contact email is axderone@gmail.com. Our registered address is Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.

2. What Axder Does

Axder is a mobile application for Android that helps business owners manage client conversations, bookings, payments and their sales pipeline. The app reads incoming messages from WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and other messaging platforms, generates AI-powered replies, and displays business data in a dashboard on the owner's phone.

3. Two Kinds of People — Business Owners and Their Customers

This policy distinguishes between two roles. The Business Owner is the person who installs Axder and runs it on their phone. The Customer is any person who sends a message to the Business Owner's phone and receives an AI-generated reply. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023), the Business Owner is the data controller for their customers' data. Axder acts as a data processor on the Business Owner's behalf. The Business Owner is responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis to process their customers' messages through Axder and for informing their customers that automated replies may be in use.

4. What Stays on the Device

The following data is stored only on the Business Owner's phone and is never transmitted to Axder or any third party: client names and phone numbers; conversation history; booking and appointment records; payment records and transaction history; product and service listings; pipeline stages and deal values; notes and documents; business profile information including business name, hours and pricing.

5. What Leaves the Device — the AI Processing Path

To generate a reply, Axder sends the following data from the device to our Cloudflare Worker proxy and then to the Anthropic API: the text of the incoming message; the client's first name; relevant business context (service names, prices, available times). This data is used only to generate the reply. It is not stored on any server after processing. It is not used to train AI models. Anthropic's standard API terms prohibit use of API inputs for model training. Our Cloudflare Worker does not log message content.

6. Account and Subscription Data

When you use Axder, we store your account token, subscription tier, message usage count and billing period in our Cloudflare KV store. This data is used only to enforce your plan limits and is not sold or shared with third parties.

7. Cloud Backup (Business Plan)

Business plan subscribers may enable encrypted cloud backup. Before leaving your device, your backup is encrypted with a key derived from your chosen password. We store only the encrypted data and cannot read its contents. If you lose your password, your backup cannot be recovered. You may disable backup at any time in Settings.

8. Payment Processing

Axder does not process payments directly. Subscription payments are handled by third-party payment processors. We do not store your card number or bank details. Axder reads incoming payment alert notifications from your bank apps on your device to detect when a customer has paid. These notifications are processed on-device and the payment amounts and sender names are stored locally only.

9. Data Retention

All client data stored on your device remains there until you delete the app or use the Reset All Data option in Settings. Usage and billing data in our systems is retained for 12 months. If you request deletion, email us at axderone@gmail.com and we will delete any server-side records within 30 days.

10. Lawful Disclosure

We do not voluntarily share data with law enforcement or government agencies. If compelled by a valid Nigerian court order, we may be required to disclose account-level information (subscription status, usage counts). We cannot provide message content or client records because we do not hold them — they exist only on the Business Owner's device.

11. Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. The app offers an in-app password lock. We follow industry-standard security practices for our Cloudflare infrastructure.

12. Children

Axder accounts must be held by a person aged 18 or over. Minors may use the app under the supervision of a parent or guardian who holds the account, for example to manage a small business. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

13. Third-Party Services

Axder uses the following third-party services: Anthropic (AI reply generation — anthropic.com); Cloudflare (proxy and usage tracking — cloudflare.com). These services have their own privacy policies. Message text passes through Anthropic's API ephemerally and is not retained or used for training.

14. Your Rights Under NDPA 2023

As a data subject under Nigerian law, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at axderone@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes via the app. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
